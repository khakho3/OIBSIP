01 / The work

Made with vision.
Built for Ghana.

Apostle Dr. Kwadwo Safo Kantanka was born in 1948 in Ghana’s Ashanti Region. Drawn early to practical problem-solving, he trained in welding at the Ghana Technical Works Institute in Kumasi and gained hands-on experience as a welder. That foundation stayed visible throughout a life devoted to making things work.

In 1969, he began a prayer group that grew into the Kristo Asafo Mission. The mission became a place where faith, technical curiosity, and community work met. Over time, its activities extended beyond worship into workshops and enterprises focused on practical skills and local production.

Safo founded Kantanka Automobile in 1994, and vehicle production followed in 1998. The company became a prominent symbol of Ghanaian participation in automotive manufacturing, assembling and developing vehicles intended for local roads and regional needs.

His legacy is larger than a badge on a vehicle. As an inventor, pastor, entrepreneur, and advocate for homegrown industry, Kantanka encouraged a generation to see technical ability as a tool for self-reliance—and to imagine Ghana not only as a market, but as a maker.

“A beacon of hope for many.”— Kantanka family statement, 2025
